Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Tidyspace keeps only what a job needs, keeps it on Atlassian’s platform, and deletes it after 90 days.

1. Who we are

Tidyspace is published by Corentin Regnier (“we”). Contact for any privacy question: tidyspace.support@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Tidyspace app for Confluence Cloud and this website.

2. In short

  • Tidyspace is an Atlassian Forge app that runs entirely on Atlassian’s infrastructure. It has no server of its own and makes no connection outside Atlassian.
  • The data a job needs is stored in the app’s storage within your Confluence site’s Atlassian environment. We, the publisher, have no access to it.
  • It is deleted 90 days after the job’s last activity, and within a week for any person whose Atlassian account is closed.
  • Tidyspace does not read the body of your pages, does not track people, uses no cookies or analytics, and sells nothing to anyone.

3. Data processed by the app

Tidyspace processes data from your Confluence site only to carry out the jobs that your administrators prepare and run.

4. Where the data is stored

Stored data is kept in Atlassian Forge hosted storage, scoped to your site’s installation of the app, encrypted at rest, and held in the same location as your Confluence data (Forge storage follows Confluence data residency). It is processed by Atlassian as the provider of the Forge platform, under Atlassian’s own terms and privacy policy. Apart from the account report described in section 6, Tidyspace sends no data anywhere else.

Inside your site, the details of a job (titles, selection, journal, export) are shown only to the administrator who prepared it; other Confluence administrators see its status and counts.

5. How long the data is kept

  • Job data (everything marked “stored” above) is deleted 90 days after the job’s last activity. Atlassian’s platform removes expired data within 48 hours after that. The job then disappears from the history; export its journal beforehand if you need a longer record.
  • Temporary data used to prepare a preview is deleted as soon as the preview is ready, and in any case after 90 days.
  • When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes all its data for your site after a retention period of 28 days, during which it can be restored at your request.
  • Deleted data may remain in Atlassian’s backups for a limited time, under Atlassian’s data retention policy.

6. Closed and changed accounts

Atlassian requires apps that store personal data to check it regularly against the accounts it belongs to. Once a week, Tidyspace sends Atlassian, through Atlassian’s personal data reporting service, the list of account identifiers it stores, each with the date the app obtained the data. Nothing else is sent: no name, no title, no content. Atlassian answers which of these accounts were closed or changed.

  • Closed account: the app erases its identifier and display name from every job, replacing them with “closed account”. A job not yet run that involves this account is stopped; it can be re-run without that person. A job being processed is erased as soon as it stops, at the latest at the next weekly check.
  • Changed account: the app reads the display name again from Confluence and stores the new one; if it cannot be read, the stored name is erased.

These changes do not extend how long a job is kept.

7. Diagnostic logs and usage metrics

When an operation fails, the app writes a technical log entry on the Forge platform: the function concerned, the error type, an error code and the HTTP status, and for an unexpected error its technical message; the weekly account check logs only counts. These entries do not include account identifiers, page titles, queries or the content of Confluence responses. The platform also records anonymous counters (for example, the number of items processed or of failed jobs) and invocation statistics. Atlassian makes these logs and metrics available to us to operate and fix the app, and keeps them under its own retention policy.

8. Data you send us

If you contact support, we use your name, email address and message only to answer you, and keep the conversation for up to 2 years after the last message, unless the law requires otherwise. Do not send us the content of your pages: we never need it.

9. This website

This website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

10. Roles and legal basis

For the data in your Confluence site, your organization decides what Tidyspace processes, through the jobs its administrators prepare: it is the controller of that data. Tidyspace processes it only to carry out those jobs. The Data Processing Agreement sets out our obligations as processor. For support requests, we are the controller and rely on our legitimate interest in answering you.

No data is sold, shared for advertising, or used for automated decision-making.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, people have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection. For data in a Confluence site, contact the administrators of that site: they control the jobs, and the data is deleted automatically after 90 days, when the account is closed, or when the app is uninstalled. For data you sent us, write to tidyspace.support@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

12. Changes

We will publish any change to this policy on this page, with its date. Significant changes, such as a new type of data or a longer retention period, will be announced in the app’s release notes on the Atlassian Marketplace before they take effect.
