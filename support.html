Support

Questions, problems or suggestions about Tidyspace: write to us.

Contact

Email: tidyspace.support@gmail.com

We answer in English or in French, on business days (Monday to Friday, Central European Time). We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Before writing

  • The documentation explains each message of the preview and each result of the journal, and has a troubleshooting section.
  • If Confluence itself is slow or failing, check status.atlassian.com.

What to include

  • your Confluence site address (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net);
  • the action of the job and its status (for example “Replace a label, Interrupted”), and the time it happened, with your time zone;
  • the message shown on screen, copied or as a screenshot;
  • for failed items, the reason shown in the journal. If you attach the CSV export, remove the titles you do not want to share: we never need the content of your pages.
Never send passwords or API tokens. Tidyspace support never asks for them, and does not need access to your site to investigate: the app runs entirely on Atlassian’s platform and its diagnostic logs contain no content.

Security issues

To report a vulnerability, follow the security page instead.

Feature requests

Suggestions are welcome at the same address. Tell us what you are trying to do on your site: the problem matters more than the solution.
