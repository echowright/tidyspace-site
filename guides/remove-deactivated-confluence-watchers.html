How to remove deactivated users from Confluence page watchers

Departed colleagues often stay on watcher lists. Here is how to clean them up across many pages.

The problem

Watcher lists are managed page by page. When accounts are deactivated or closed, they can remain on the pages they watched, and cleaning them up means opening each page.

Steps with Tidyspace

You need to be a Confluence administrator and have Tidyspace installed.

  1. Open Confluence administration, then Apps, then Tidyspace.
  2. Choose Watchers, then the option to remove watchers who are deactivated or closed accounts.
  3. Select the content (spaces, pages, a label or a CQL query) and click Prepare the preview.
  4. Check the preview, then Run.
Confluence only lets an app change another person’s watch status if the app has administrator rights. If watcher changes fail with “Permission denied”, give the app Tidyspace the space administration permission in the space settings (or the site-wide equivalent), then re-run the job.

You can also add or remove specific users (up to 10 per job). Details in documentation, Watchers.

Verify

The panel shows what changed and what failed; the CSV export keeps the full record. Pages you cannot access are excluded and only counted.
