How to rename or merge a label across a whole Confluence site

A label is spelled two ways, or a team renamed a project. Here is how to fix every page in one job, without editing pages one by one.

The problem

Labels drift. q3-roadmap and roadmap-q3 end up on different pages, a product is renamed, or a label is simply misspelled. Confluence lets you add or remove a label on a page, but to my knowledge it has no built-in way to rename a label across every space of a site. Editing each page by hand does not scale beyond a few dozen pages.

Steps with Tidyspace

You need to be a Confluence administrator and have Tidyspace installed from the Atlassian Marketplace.

  1. In Confluence, open Confluence administration, then Apps, then Tidyspace.
  2. Choose Labels, then the action Replace a label (rename or merge).
  3. Enter the current label and the new label. Labels are lowercase, with no spaces, as in Confluence.
  4. Leave Across the whole site checked to cover every space, or uncheck it and select only some spaces or pages.
  5. Click Prepare the preview. Nothing is written yet.

Check the preview

The preview lists how many items will be processed and the first items of the selection. It also tells you about two cases to know before you run:

  • Pages you cannot access are excluded and only counted. Tidyspace never lets you change content your own account could not edit.
  • Restricted pages the app cannot see are listed with the fix (add the app through Share), so you can include them.

Run, then verify

Click Run. On each page, Tidyspace adds the new label, then removes the old one only once the new one is in place. If the new label already exists on a page, the two are simply merged. Jobs run in batches of 50, pause by themselves when Confluence rate limits are reached, and can be resumed or cancelled.

When the job ends, failed or skipped pages are shown in the panel and all of them are in the CSV export.

Good to know

  • Search results can lag a few minutes after a change, so Tidyspace uses its own log as the reference, not a new search.
  • A job handles up to 20,000 pages you have access to; above that it refuses to start rather than truncating.
  • Details and limits: documentation, Labels and Limits.
