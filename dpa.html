Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This agreement sets out how Tidyspace processes personal data on your behalf, as GDPR article 28 requires. It forms part of the Terms.

1. Parties and roles

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) is between the organization that installs and uses the Tidyspace app (“you”, the controller) and Corentin Regnier, publisher of Tidyspace (“we”, the processor). It applies when your use of Tidyspace involves personal data covered by the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”) or similar laws. It takes effect when you install the app and ends when the app is uninstalled and its data has been deleted.

This DPA covers only the data of your Confluence site that the app processes to carry out your jobs. For support requests that you send us, we are the controller, as described in the privacy policy.

2. Subject matter and details of the processing

Subject matterBulk actions on labels, watchers and page restrictions in your Confluence Cloud site, with a preview, a journal and an export.
Nature and purposeReading the content and permission data a job needs, storing the job (selection, preview, results) in the app’s storage, applying the change you confirm, and showing and exporting the journal. Nothing else.
DurationWhile a job exists: it is deleted 90 days after its last activity. Data of a person whose Atlassian account is closed is erased within a week. On uninstallation, Atlassian deletes the app’s data for your site after 28 days.
Categories of dataAccount identifiers and display names of users chosen as watchers or in restrictions; identifiers and names of groups; account identifier of the administrator who prepared or started the job; identifiers, titles, types and space keys of the selected pages and blog posts; results of each item (outcome and reason code). We do not process the body of pages, attachments or comments, email addresses, passwords or tokens.
Data subjectsYour Confluence administrators, users and groups of your site, and, through page titles, the people named in them.

3. Your instructions

We process personal data only on your documented instructions, which are the jobs your administrators prepare and run in the app, the settings of the app, and this DPA. If we believe an instruction breaks the law, we will tell you. The app runs only for your site’s administrators and never widens a person’s own access: content a person cannot view or edit is excluded from their job.

4. Confidentiality and access

The data stored by the app is held in Atlassian’s platform for your installation. We have no access to it. Inside your site, the details of a job are visible only to the administrator who prepared it. Anyone acting on our behalf is bound by confidentiality.

5. Security

We apply the measures described on the security page, in particular: the app has no server of its own and makes no connection outside Atlassian; storage is encrypted at rest by the Atlassian platform; every action is restricted to Confluence administrators and checked on the server; scopes are limited to what each call needs; technical logs contain no account identifier, title or page content; stored job data expires after 90 days; dependencies are monitored and vulnerabilities fixed within the deadlines set by Atlassian.

6. Sub-processors

You authorize Atlassian as the only sub-processor. It provides the Forge platform on which the app runs and stores its data (compute, storage, queue), under its own terms and privacy policy, and in the location of your Confluence data (Atlassian data residency). We use no other sub-processor for your data. We will announce any change in the app’s release notes on the Atlassian Marketplace at least 30 days before it takes effect; if you object for a legitimate reason, you can uninstall the app.

7. International transfers

We transfer no data ourselves. Storage and processing by Atlassian follow your Confluence data location, and any transfer outside the European Economic Area by Atlassian is governed by your agreement with Atlassian.

8. Assistance

Since we cannot access the data, requests from people exercising their rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection) are handled by your administrators; the data is also deleted automatically as described above. We will help you, within reason, to answer such requests, to carry out an impact assessment, and to deal with a regulator, if you write to us.

9. Personal data breach

If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify Atlassian within 24 hours, and you without undue delay and within 72 hours wherever possible, saying what happened, which data was concerned, what we did and what you should do (see the security page).

10. Deletion and return

You can export the journal of a job as a CSV file before it expires. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the data after its retention period of 28 days. We keep no copy of your data.

11. Information and audits

On written request, we will give you the information needed to check that we meet this DPA. Because the app runs entirely on Atlassian, you can also rely on the certifications and reports Atlassian publishes for its platform. An audit of our own organization can be done once a year, with 30 days’ notice, at your cost, and without access to other customers’ information.

12. Liability and law

The liability provisions and the governing law of the Terms apply to this DPA. Where this DPA and the Terms differ on the processing of personal data, this DPA prevails.

13. Contact

For any question about this DPA: tidyspace.support@gmail.com.
